Getting an answer is the easy part now.

Any model can read a document and tell you something plausible. None of them can tell you what they missed, why they decided what they decided, or hand you something that holds up when opposing counsel, a regulator or a judge pulls at it. That gap is where the risk sits, and it is the whole reason graphlion exists.

One matter, start to finish

Not a research tool bolted onto a review platform. The answers and the review machinery are the same product.

01

Ask

Put a question to the matter. graphlion plans the search, builds a working set and answers from it, with every source cited.

02

Searches

Working sets are saved, named and reusable. Each one keeps the record of exactly how it was built.

03

Review

Reviewers code batches for relevance, privilege and issues. The AI suggests, the lawyer decides, and every override is logged.

04

Privilege

Produce, withhold or refer to the client, scoped to relevant documents only, with the basis recorded as you go.

05

People and organisations

Aliases, misspellings and multiple addresses resolved to one person, with their whole chronology in one place.

06

Produce

Export the production set and the methodology behind it, together.

Every classification comes with its own audit

graphlion never shows a confidence score without a route to the evidence behind it. Open any AI decision and you get the same four things.

Elusion test
A sample drawn from outside the set, the misses found in it, and an estimated recall, so you know what the search did not catch, not just what it did.
Confidence
Banded, never a bare number, and always paired with the documents that produced it.
Override history
Every human correction, and the reviewer-agreement rate that follows from them.
The exact prompt
The instruction that was applied, and its version. No black box to explain away.

All of it accumulates into a continuous methodology record: collection, search terms, de-duplication, AI seed, continuous active learning, QC, privilege, production. When someone asks how the exercise was run, it is one export rather than a fortnight of reconstruction.
